# Fina 1.17

Date: 2026-09-10

## App Store — What’s New

- Track business expenses in dedicated business workspaces.
- Add receipts from your camera, photo library, or files and review business-use amounts.
- Exclude a transaction once or always exclude future transactions from the same merchant, with clear labels for each choice.
- Improved workspace switching and transaction reliability.

## User-Facing Changes

- Business workspaces now include an Expenses tab with receipt and evidence workflows.
- Receipt uploads support itemized and payment-proof documents.
- Budget exclusions can apply to one transaction or future transactions from the merchant.
- Repeating exclusions are labeled “Always excluded”; one-time exclusions are labeled “Excluded once.”

## TestFlight — What to Test

- Create or switch to a business workspace and open Expenses.
- Upload a receipt from each available source and review its transaction link and business-use amount.
- Exclude a transaction once and confirm it shows “Excluded once.”
- Always exclude a merchant and confirm later matching transactions are excluded automatically.
- Include a transaction again and verify the selected merchant scope is respected.
