# Fina 1.13

Date: 2026-07-31

## App Store — What’s New

- Bank connections now stay available for 30 days after moving to the Free plan, making it easier to upgrade again without reconnecting.
- Added a clear bank-access expiration notice during the grace period.
- Improved transaction syncing to prevent duplicates when reconnecting a previously linked bank.
- Improved reliability around plan changes and bank connection cleanup.

## User-Facing Changes

- Free plan grace period
  - Existing bank connections remain linked for 30 days after a paid plan ends.
  - Banks appear disabled during the grace period with a clear expiration date and upgrade guidance.
  - New bank transactions can continue syncing securely in the background but remain hidden on Free.
  - Upgrading during the grace period restores normal access without requiring the bank to be connected again.
  - If the grace period expires, the bank connection and grace-period-only transaction data are removed.
- Bank status messaging
  - Home shows the bank expiration toolbar only while an existing connection is in an active grace period.
  - The toolbar is not shown to people who never connected a bank or after the grace period has expired.
- Plaid reliability
  - Reconnect matching now uses Plaid transaction identity and legacy account matching to prevent duplicate imports.
  - Billing cleanup runs independently from Morning Brief processing.

## Operations

- Added a manual welcome-email command for customer support.
- Added dry-run and hard-delete admin tooling for high-confidence reconnect duplicates.
- Added a dedicated scheduled Plaid billing reconciliation job.
